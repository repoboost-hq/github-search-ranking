# Durability and detection

## Why patterns get flagged

GitHub's own research - covered by WIRED in 2023 - found that spikes from fresh, empty accounts are flagged and removed. Research on fake-star campaigns (CMU/Socket, 2024-2025) reached the same conclusion: new throw-away accounts and stars that land synchronously in one short window are the two signatures detection looks for.

## How this documentation's method differs

- Actions come from accounts with years of public commit history.
- Delivery is spread across a 12-24 hour window, never one burst.
- Signals grow together - stars, forks, watches and followers - so the profile reads as discovery, not a single number moving.

## Honest limits

No provider can promise permanence - GitHub can change its systems at any time. What can be promised is the most durable method that exists today: real accounts, real pacing, real history.
