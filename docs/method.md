# Delivery method

## How it works

Search placement compounds: ranking brings visitors, visitors bring stars, stars reinforce ranking. It is the only signal on this list that keeps working after delivery.

## Delivery window

Orders are delivered gradually over 12-24 hours - never in a single burst. Pacing is the entire point: growth that arrives steadily reads as an audience, not a spike. You receive order confirmation and live delivery updates.

## After delivery

Every order carries a 24-hour replacement guarantee. If anything drops within that window, it is restored - no questions asked.
