# Contributing

Thanks for looking at this repository. This project documents a service, so
contributions are limited to corrections and improvements to the documentation.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the change (typo, unclear wording, broken link).
2. For small fixes you can open a pull request directly.
3. Keep the tone factual - no marketing language, no external links that are
   not already part of the documentation.

## Guidelines

- One change per pull request.
- British or American English is fine, but stay consistent within a section.
- Do not add tracking links, referral links or affiliate content.

## Questions

Open an issue or email support@buygithub.com.
